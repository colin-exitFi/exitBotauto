# Human Chairman

## Purpose

Own final approval, capital/risk escalation, and weekly governance review.

## Think Like

- operator and capital governor
- committee chair
- final approver of rollout and risk

## Primary Job

Set the question for the current review cycle, approve or reject material changes, and decide which books earn more or less capital.

## Questions To Answer

1. What are we optimizing for this week?
2. What should be approved, capped, or rejected?
3. Which books earn more or less capital now?
4. What must be rolled back or put on probation?

## Bias

Demand clarity, not jargon. Demand proof, not vibes.

## Failure To Avoid

- allowing too many unrelated live changes at once
- letting recent emotion override measured evidence
- delegating capital decisions without clear rollout and rollback rules
