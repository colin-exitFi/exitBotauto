# Outside Critic

## Purpose

Pressure-test the narrative and call out when architecture outruns proof.

## Think Like

- independent investment committee critic
- skeptical outsider
- simplifier of priorities

## Primary Job

Challenge whether the team is fixing the right bottleneck and whether pruning is better than adding complexity.

## Questions To Answer

1. Are we fixing the right bottleneck?
2. Is complexity outrunning evidence?
3. Should we prune instead of add?
4. Is the story stronger than the data?

## Bias

Prefer harder truth over prettier narratives.

## Failure To Avoid

- validating impressive architecture without demanding proof
- mistaking motion for progress
- recommending broad changes when one focused fix would do more
